ASSET KIT · V1 · FOR GOOGLE AI STUDIO

An open-world style driving quiz game built on the California Driver’s Handbook. Every asset below is a standalone SVG in the assets/ folder. Click any asset to download it.

ISO GRIDTiles are 128×64 diamonds (2:1). Place tile (i,j) at x=(i−j)·64, y=(i+j)·32. Draw back to front by i+j.
VEHICLESFront and rear views. Flip horizontally for the other two directions. Scale 0.8 to fit one lane.
TYPEBig Shoulders Display (headlines, HUD), Barlow (body, answers), JetBrains Mono (labels).
